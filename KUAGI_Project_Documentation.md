# KUAGI AI Accessibility Gap Explorer

## Map<>Kathon 2026 Project

## Developer
NSABIMANA Fabrice

## Overview

KUAGI AI Accessibility Gap Explorer is an AI-powered GIS dashboard that identifies and visualizes accessibility gaps across Kigali City.

The project combines OpenStreetMap data, GIS analysis, and AI-assisted insights to support better urban planning decisions.

## Public Good Problem

Many communities experience unequal access to essential services such as:

- Schools
- Health facilities
- Markets
- Public transport
- Roads

KUAGI helps identify areas where accessibility improvements are most needed.

## OSM Data Used

OpenStreetMap contributed:

- Road networks
- Schools
- Health facilities
- Markets
- Transport locations
- Urban features

## Methodology

1. Collect OpenStreetMap data.
2. Prepare and analyze spatial layers.
3. Calculate accessibility indicators.
4. Generate KUAGI scores.
5. Classify areas by priority.
6. Provide AI-assisted recommendations.

## Priority Classification

| Priority | Score |
|---|---|
| Critical | 80-100 |
| High | 60-79.99 |
| Moderate | 40-59.99 |
| Low | 0-39.99 |
