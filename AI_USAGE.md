# AI Usage Disclosure

## AI Tools Used

- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Dify
- GPT-5

## Role of AI

AI was used to support the development and enhancement of KUAGI through:

- Developing and improving ArcGIS Arcade expressions
- Improving project documentation
- Generating and refining accessibility explanations
- Supporting recommended intervention ideas
- Reviewing project structure and workflow
- Assisting with the development of the KUAGI AI accessibility assistant
- Supporting natural language interaction with the KUAGI accessibility dataset

## KUAGI AI Assistant

KUAGI includes an integrated AI assistant that allows users to ask natural language questions about the project's accessibility analysis.

The assistant uses the available KUAGI accessibility dataset as a knowledge source to support responses related to:

- Accessibility gaps
- KUAGI Scores
- Gap Priority
- Districts and sectors
- Health accessibility
- Education accessibility
- Market accessibility
- Transport and road accessibility
- Infrastructure conditions
- Population-related indicators
- Recommended interventions

The AI assistant retrieves relevant information from the KUAGI dataset before generating responses for project-related questions.

## Validation

All GIS analysis, project outputs, and AI-assisted content were reviewed by the developer before inclusion in the project.

AI-generated responses are intended to support interpretation and decision-making and should be considered alongside the underlying GIS data and spatial analysis.

No AI-generated edits were directly uploaded to OpenStreetMap.
